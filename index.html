Labour & Employment Laws

India’s new Labour Codes

India has consolidated 29 central labour laws into four Labour Codes, creating a more unified framework for wages, industrial relations, workplace safety and social security. The framework changes important compliance responsibilities for employers and establishments.

Code on Wages

Covers minimum wages, payment of wages and bonuses, wage-related definitions and equal remuneration principles.

Industrial Relations Code

Deals with industrial disputes, trade unions, standing orders, retrenchment, layoffs, closure and other employment-relations matters.

Occupational Safety, Health & Working Conditions Code

Provides the framework for workplace safety, health, welfare, working conditions, registration and related employer obligations.

Code on Social Security

Brings together social-security provisions including EPF, ESI, gratuity, maternity benefits and a framework extending to gig and platform workers.

What this means for employers

Businesses should review workforce records, wage structures, registrations, social-security obligations, policies and workplace compliance. Exact requirements can depend on the establishment, workforce, applicable rules and state-level provisions.

Electronic compliance
Registration, records and filings are increasingly standardised and digitised.
Revised wage concepts
Definitions can affect payroll, benefits, gratuity and social-security calculations.
Stronger compliance exposure
Policies and procedures should be reviewed to reduce regulatory and legal risk.

Other Laws & Legal Awareness

Rights every Indian should know.

A public-awareness section covering important everyday rights and legal protections. It is general information and not a substitute for case-specific legal advice.

Right to EqualityConstitutional protection against unlawful discrimination and unequal treatment by the State, subject to recognised constitutional provisions.
Right to Life & Personal LibertyProtection of life and personal liberty except according to procedure established by law, interpreted through extensive constitutional jurisprudence.
Right to Constitutional RemediesConstitutional remedies are available for enforcement of fundamental rights through appropriate courts.
Consumer RightsRemedies may be available for defective goods, deficient services, unfair trade practices and misleading representations.
Women & Maternity ProtectionsIndian law provides protections concerning maternity benefits, workplace rights and specified safeguards for women.
Free Legal AidEligible persons may access legal services under the statutory legal-services framework, subject to applicable criteria.
FIR & Criminal ProcedureCriminal procedure is now governed principally by the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, with rights and procedures depending on the facts of the case.
Current Criminal LawsIndia’s current criminal-law framework includes the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita and Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam.
Workplace RightsEmployees and workers may have rights relating to wages, safety, social security, maternity benefits, employment conditions and dispute resolution.
