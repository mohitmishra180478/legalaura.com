Labour & Employment Laws

India’s four Labour Codes.

India consolidated 29 central labour laws into four Labour Codes, creating a more unified framework for wages, industrial relations, workplace safety and social security. Employers should review the detailed rules and state-level requirements applicable to their establishment.

Code on Wages, 2019

Minimum wages, payment of wages and bonuses, wage-related definitions and equal-remuneration principles.

Industrial Relations Code, 2020

Industrial disputes, trade unions, standing orders, retrenchment, layoffs, closure and related employment-relations issues.

Occupational Safety, Health & Working Conditions Code, 2020

Workplace safety, health, welfare, working conditions, registrations and related employer duties.

Code on Social Security, 2020

Social-security provisions including EPF, ESI, gratuity, maternity benefits and framework coverage relevant to gig and platform workers.

Key employer compliance themes

Businesses may need to review registrations, workforce records, wage structures, social-security obligations, policies and workplace compliance. Exact obligations depend on the applicable Code, rules, state provisions and establishment.

Electronic registration & records
Greater standardisation and digitisation of statutory processes.
Standardised wage definitions
Potential impact on payroll, benefits, gratuity, severance and social-security calculations.
Stronger enforcement
Higher compliance exposure makes timely review of processes and documentation important.
Legal Awareness

Rights every Indian should know.

A practical public-awareness section covering selected rights and everyday legal protections. This is general information, not a substitute for advice on a specific case.

Right to EqualityConstitutional protection against unlawful discrimination and unequal treatment by the State, subject to recognised constitutional exceptions.
Right to Life & Personal LibertyArticle 21 protects life and personal liberty except according to procedure established by law.
FIR & Zero FIRFor cognizable offences, information may be given to the police. Zero FIR enables registration irrespective of territorial jurisdiction, with transfer to the appropriate police station thereafter.
Legal AidEligible persons may seek free legal services through legal-services authorities under the Legal Services Authorities Act framework.
Consumer ProtectionConsumers may seek remedies for defective goods, deficient services, unfair trade practices and certain misleading representations under consumer-protection law.
Maternity & Workplace ProtectionsEligible employees may have statutory rights relating to maternity benefits and workplace protections, depending on the applicable establishment and legal requirements.
Current Criminal LawsIndia’s present criminal-law framework includes the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita and Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam.
Access to Courts & RemediesConstitutional and statutory remedies may be available through courts, tribunals and other authorities depending on the nature of the dispute.
Workplace RightsWorkers and employees may have rights relating to wages, safety, social security, employment conditions, maternity benefits and dispute resolution.
